# TinySurprise

**A little more personal than a regular message.**

TinySurprise is a creative app for making interactive surprises for friends, partners, family, or anyone you want to surprise.

Instead of sending a regular message or photo, you can turn your memories, stories, and inside jokes into something people can actually interact with.

Whether it's for a birthday, anniversary, special occasion, or just because.

## What Can You Make?

TinySurprise comes with seven interactive experiences:

- **Catch the Heart:** Catch falling hearts to reveal photos and messages.
- **Photo Puzzle:** Put together a photo to uncover a surprise.
- **Letters for Every Moment:** Create letters for different moods and occasions.
- **Choose Our Date:** Make choosing your next date a little more interesting.
- **Memory Quiz:** Challenge someone with questions about your shared memories.
- **Our Tiny Storybook:** Tell a story through photos, chapters, and personal notes.
- **The Cozy Playlist:** Put together a collection of songs, memories, and messages.

## Make It Your Own

Customize your surprise with photos, messages, colors, backgrounds, animations, and music.

You can preview everything before sending it, and the person receiving it can open the surprise directly in their browser without installing the app.

## Simple and Private

TinySurprise doesn't require an account. Your creations are stored locally on your device, and you choose what to share.

You can also save an editable backup to revisit your creations later.
